# MochiMeter - Beta App Description

## Short Description (80 characters)
AI-powered calorie tracking. Snap a photo, get instant nutrition info.

## TestFlight Description

**MochiMeter** makes calorie tracking effortless with AI-powered food recognition. Simply snap a photo of your meal and let our AI instantly estimate calories and macros - no manual searching required.

### Key Features

**AI Food Scanner**
Point your camera at any meal and get instant nutritional estimates. Our AI recognises foods, portions, and provides accurate calorie and macro breakdowns in seconds.

**Barcode Scanner**
Scan packaged foods to instantly pull nutrition data from our extensive database of over 2 million products.

**Smart Search**
Search from multiple food databases including community-verified foods, USDA data, and OpenFoodFacts. Results are ranked by relevance and accuracy.

**Daily Tracking**
- Set personalised calorie and macro goals
- Track water intake
- Monitor your progress with visual charts
- View weekly and monthly reports

**Fasting Timer**
Built-in intermittent fasting tracker to help you stay on schedule with your eating windows.

**Cloud Sync**
Your data syncs securely across all your devices. Log on your phone, view on your iPad.

**Home Screen Widgets**
Quick-glance widgets show your daily progress without opening the app.
