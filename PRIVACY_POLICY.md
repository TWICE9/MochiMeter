# Privacy Policy for MochiMeter

**Last Updated: December 17, 2024**

## Introduction

MochiMeter ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application MochiMeter (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

For terms governing your use of the Website and App, see our Terms of Service: `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md` (also published at https://mochimeter.com.au/terms/).

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials.
- **Profile Information**: Name, profile picture, and dietary preferences you choose to provide.
- **Food Logs**: Food items you log, including names, nutritional information, photos, timestamps, and meal categories.
- **Health Goals**: Calorie targets, macro goals, water intake goals, and fasting preferences you set.
- **Recipes**: Custom recipes you create within the App.
- **Reminders**: Meal and hydration reminder preferences.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
- **Usage Data**: Features used, interaction patterns, and App performance data.
- **Photos**: Only photos you explicitly choose to capture or upload for food logging. We do not access your photo library without your permission.

### Information from Third-Party Services

- **Food Databases**: When you search for foods, we query external databases (OpenFoodFacts, USDA) to provide nutritional information. These queries do not include personal identifiers.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
- Sync your data across devices when you're signed in
- Process and analyse food photos using AI to estimate nutritional content
- Personalise your experience and provide relevant food suggestions
- Send notifications and reminders you've configured
- Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
- Analyse usage patterns to improve the App
- Process in-app purchases and subscriptions

## Data Storage and Security

### Cloud Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides:
- Encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL)
- Encrypted data at rest
- Secure authentication protocols
- Data centres with industry-standard security measures

### Local Storage

Some data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage frameworks (SwiftData) for offline access and performance.

### Data Retention

- Your data is retained while your account is active
- You can delete your account and associated data at any time through the App settings
- After account deletion, your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- Purpose: User authentication and cloud data storage

### Superwall

## Your Rights and Choices

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Apple's requirements, you have the right to:

### Access and Portability
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your food logs and health data

### Correction
- Update or correct your personal information through the App

### Deletion
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Remove individual food logs or recipes

### Opt-Out
- Disable cloud sync and use the App in offline mode
- Disable notifications
- Revoke camera or photo library permissions

### Data Minimisation
- Use the App without creating an account (limited functionality)
- Choose not to provide optional information

## Children's Privacy
